# Family Portal Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** November 10, 2026

Family Portal is a private household calendar display used only by the Carter family. This policy explains what Google data the app uses and how it is handled.

## What data we access

Family Portal requests one Google permission:

- **Google Calendar events** (`https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.events`)

We use it only to **read and create events on our own family's Google calendars**. We do not access any other Google data.

## How data is used

- Calendar data is shown only on our home screen and our family's devices.
- The sign-in token is stored only on our home server.
- Calendar data is not used for any other purpose.

## What we never do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not share data with third parties.
- We do not use data for advertising.
- We do not use data to train AI or machine learning models.

## Google API Services User Data Policy

Family Portal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

## Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time from your Google Account at [myaccount.google.com/permissions](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Deleting the token from our home server also stops all access.
